Enrollment FAQ

Answers to the questions prospective students ask most. Open a question to read its answer.

What are the admission requirements for enrolling in a degree program?

To enroll in a degree program, you typically need to meet these requirements:

  • A high school diploma or equivalent (e.g., GED)
  • Completion of prerequisite courses, such as math and science for engineering
  • Letters of recommendation and a personal statement
  • A minimum GPA, which varies by program

How do I submit my application?

Applications are submitted online through the admissions portal.

When is the application deadline?

Deadlines vary by program; check the dates for your program.

Can I defer my admission?

Admitted students may defer for up to one year by writing to the admissions office before May 1.
