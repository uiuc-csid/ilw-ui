Graduate admissions

The department admits students to the MS and PhD programs each fall. Read the requirements before you apply, and see the Graduate College (opens in a new tab) for university-wide rules. Deadlines are firm.

Requirements

Every application needs the following:

  • A statement of purpose
  • Three letters of recommendation
    • at least two from faculty
    • submitted by the recommenders themselves
  • Transcripts from every institution attended

How to apply

  1. Create an account in the application system.
  2. Fill in each section and upload your documents.
  3. Pay the fee and submit.

Fee waivers

Waivers are available for McNair Scholars and for applicants with financial need.

Eligibility

Ask the graduate office before you submit; waivers can't be applied afterwards.

Note

Fee waivers do not carry over between terms.
